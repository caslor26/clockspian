How to use it

  1. 💻Open it on the laptop

    Open Clockspian in a browser tab on the small screen beside your monitors.

  2. ⌨️Press F

    Click the clock and press F. Everything around it slides away, leaving just the clock, edge to edge. Press it again to bring it all back.

  3. ⚙️Make it yours

    Move the mouse and a gear appears top right, with themes, light and dark, size and a countdown. Your browser asks where you are so the weather is local.
