👋 Hello. This is a clock.

It’s for your laptop screen, open beside your monitors all day and showing your wallpaper to nobody. Give it something to do.

💻The laptop screen

Work on the big screens. Leave Clockspian on the small one.

🤍Clean on purpose

Big numbers, quiet colours, ten themes. No account, no ads, no pings.

🌦️Just enough

The time and the date. The weather and a countdown if you want them.

⌨️The important bit

Click the clock on your laptop screen and press F11. The tabs and address bar slide away and it’s just the clock, edge to edge.

F11 again brings them back. Nothing happening? Try Fn + F11.

  • 📍Switch the weather on and your browser asks where you are, rounded to about a kilometre. Say no and you get Stockholm.
  • ⚙️Move the mouse and a gear appears top right, with themes, light and dark, size, the weather and the countdown.
  • 🎬Rather watch? Here’s the half-minute film.

⚠️ Are you sure?

With Insane mode on, this clock stops being polite the moment your countdown hits zero.

Clockspian accepts no responsibility for spilled coffee.
