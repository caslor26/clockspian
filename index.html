👋 Hello. This is a clock.

Clockspian tells you the time. It also tells you the weather, which is frankly more than most clocks bother with. That’s the whole pitch.

🖥️What it’s for

The spare monitor, the retired tablet, the TV nobody watches during the day. Open Clockspian full-screen, walk away, and let it be quietly, reliably correct about what time it is.

🌦️The weather bit

Clockspian asks for your location so the forecast is for where you actually are. Say no and you get Uppsala, Sweden — lovely, but possibly not your town. Your position is rounded to about a kilometre and only used to look up the weather and the place’s name.

⚙️Making it yours

Move the mouse or tap the screen and a small gear appears in the top-right corner. Behind it: themes, light and dark, size, and the power to stop the colon blinking if it’s started to feel judgemental. It all sticks for next time.

🤷That’s it, really

No account, no ads, no notifications. If the internet wanders off, the time keeps going and the weather holds its last reading until it’s back. It is, after all, just a clock.
